CAMPFIRE STORIES: FOREVER — STORY SOURCES

The twenty included story texts are human-written public-domain tales, not ChatGPT-written or AI-generated stories. Sources and changes are listed below. No copyright in these historical texts is claimed by Gideon.

Whitespace is reflowed into chat messages; typographic quotes/dashes are normalised, print emphasis and footnote numbers omitted. Narratives are not abridged or rewritten. No generated story premises or spoken introductions are included. A short roleplay emote from the earlier addon is restored at the explicit owner request, separately from these sourced narratives. Start story sends that action; the next manual click begins the original text.
