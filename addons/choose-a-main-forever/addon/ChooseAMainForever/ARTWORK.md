# Artwork

`Media/ChooseAMainForeverIcon.png` is the project master icon. `Media/ChooseAMainForeverIcon.tga` is the 256×256 in-game derivative used by the TOC, header and minimap button.

The emblem was generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 11 October 2026 from an original prompt describing a golden compass/sword, nine jewel points and a teal infinity ribbon. It contains no copied Warcraft logo or faction insignia.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a centered, circular, hand-painted high-fantasy game UI emblem for “Choose a Main: Forever”: a weathered golden compass rose whose needle subtly forms a sword, encircled by nine jewel-like points, with a restrained teal infinity ribbon in the lower ring. Make it readable at 32px, with aged bronze and parchment-gold materials, a warm contemplative glow, transparent outside the emblem, and no words, letters, numerals, faction symbols, copied logos or watermark.

Genuine gameplay screenshots are intentionally deferred until owner acceptance in the real Forever client.

## Mandatory CurseForge crop validation

Every CurseForge use of this artwork must follow `WORKFLOW-GATES.md`: retain safe margins, deliberately centre and expand the crop, inspect the small author-editor preview, save, reload, and inspect the actual project-list or public thumbnail at player-visible size. Clipped, off-centre, corner-only and unintended black-background crops are failed outputs and must be corrected before completion is reported.
