# Known limitations

- The owner reviewed the interface in the Forever client before authorising version 1.0.0. Automated checks cover the engine and interface construction; exhaustive character and long-session testing remains ongoing through normal public feedback.
- Forever is in beta; all 27 capability profiles are provisional and versioned for build `1.60.1.70338`.
- Ratings express qualitative playstyle compatibility, not damage, healing or threat performance.
- Paladin has less detailed official build-specific evidence than the four published paired class deep dives available during research.
- Character-sex preference is stored for the player's record but does not affect ranking because it does not constrain the verified class matrix.
- Free-text notes are stored locally and deliberately ignored by scoring.
- The engine does not know the player's skill, social group, latency, accessibility needs or future balance changes unless represented by structured answers.
- Confidence labels describe separation within this model; they are not measured prediction accuracy.
- Localisation beyond English is not included in version 1.0.0.
