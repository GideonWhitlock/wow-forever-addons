# Artwork record

`artwork/project-logo.png` was generated specifically for Gentle Reminder: Forever on 11 October 2026 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool.

Creative direction: an original painterly fantasy emblem combining an antique pocket watch and a green leaf, with a restrained forest-green, aged-gold and cream palette. It is intended to communicate calm, time and personal care at small thumbnail sizes.

The artwork contains no copied World of Warcraft logo, Blizzard mark, character, text or screenshot.

The source PNG is centre-cropped without altering its composition, circularly masked with transparent corners, reduced with high-quality resampling and a light sharpening pass, then packaged as the uncompressed 128×128 RGBA TGA at `addon/Media/Icon.tga`. Its original ring is reinforced in the outer margin with a dark separation line, an aged-gold main stroke and a warm highlight, giving roughly nine source pixels of gold edge without scaling down or covering the watch-and-leaf subject. The same texture is used for the AddOns-list metadata, minimap button, settings header and reminder card. The minimap launcher has no square backdrop and uses a circular highlight. `artwork/icon-preview-128.png` records the processed appearance, `artwork/icon-preview-minimap-upscaled.png` is a nearest-neighbour enlargement of the actual 30-pixel sampling, and `artwork/icon-preview-collector-upscaled.png` composites that exact sample onto a dark collector bar for border-weight and corner checks.

Final generation prompt:

> A square CurseForge project logo: an original, polished emblem combining a small antique pocket watch with a soft green leaf, suggesting time, calm, and a friendly personal reminder. Deep forest-green circular field, restrained warm gold border, subtle dark vignette; painterly fantasy game icon with a crisp silhouette, centered composition, warm soft glow, weathered brass and natural leaf detail. No characters, medical symbols, alarm bell, red warning color, World of Warcraft logo, copied Blizzard marks, text, letters, or watermark.
