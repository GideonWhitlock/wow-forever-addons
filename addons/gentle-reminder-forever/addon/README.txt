Gentle Reminder: Forever 1.0.1

Open settings with /grf or the pocket-watch minimap icon.
Add a one-off reminder with /grf remind 20m Your reminder text
See /grf help for all commands.

Private by design: no chat broadcast, group messages, telemetry or advertising.
Prepared and owner-accepted on WoW: Forever 1.60.1.70338, interface 16001.
