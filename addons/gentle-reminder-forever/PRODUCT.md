# Gentle Reminder: Forever — product definition

## Promise

Gentle Reminder: Forever helps a player remember their own intentions without judging, coercing, or interrupting them. It is a private prompt, not a wellness score and not a parental-control system.

The add-on can say “this is the thing you asked me to remember.” It must never say “you have played too much,” force a logout, disable controls, shame the player, contact a group, or upload activity or reminder text.

## Evidence-based MVP

- Recurring reminders with separate weekday and weekend intervals and text.
- One-off “remind me in…” timers for real-life tasks.
- Optional bedtime time and custom bedtime wording for each profile, with an explicit Local time (default) or Server time basis and visible clock comparison.
- Session and current-day logged-in totals, presented as neutral information.
- One to five configurable snooze choices.
- A warm reminder card, optional gentle sound, and opt-in local text-to-speech.
- Delivery deferred during combat, cinematics, dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and arenas. A visible card is hidden and re-queued if one starts; ordinary open-world parties remain allowed.
- Movable minimap button, slash-command recovery path, local SavedVariables, and a rolling settings backup.

## Deliberate limits

- No forced logout, input blocking, countdown punishment, streaks, goals, grades, warnings, or moral language.
- No party/raid/add-on messages, chat broadcast, telemetry, cloud sync, analytics, ads, or reminder sharing.
- No AFK detection in the MVP. “Session” and “today” mean logged-in time while the add-on is loaded.
- No calendar integration, medication advice, or medical claims. Medication can be reminder text chosen by the player, nothing more.
- No promise that text-to-speech works until verified in the live Forever client; visual delivery remains the dependable path.

## Acceptance questions for the owner

1. Does the default text feel warm and non-patronising after seeing it several times?
2. Are the default 60-minute weekday and 75-minute weekend intervals sensible, or should both begin at 60?
3. Is “Done for now” the right dismissal language?
4. Is the card noticeable without feeling like an alarm?
5. Should an overdue one-off reminder appear on the next login, or quietly expire after a grace period?
