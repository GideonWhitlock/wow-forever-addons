# Totem Tooltip: Forever

Hover over any player's Shaman totem—including your own—to see a clear explanation of the buff, debuff, damage, cleanse, or utility effect it supplies.

## Features

- Works with your totems and other players' totems.
- Covers WoW: Forever Shaman earth, fire, water, and air totems, including Mana Tide.
- Recognises every rank without showing misleading rank-specific numbers.
- Uses the game's localized spell names when available.
- Adds information directly to the normal World of Warcraft tooltip.
- Has no required libraries or dependencies.

## Commands

- `/ttf` — show the current status and help.
- `/ttf on` — enable totem information.
- `/ttf off` — disable totem information.
- `/ttf toggle` — switch the add-on on or off.

## Installation

1. Put the `TotemTooltipForever` folder in your World of Warcraft `Interface/AddOns` folder.
2. Restart the game or reload the interface.
3. Make sure **Totem Tooltip: Forever** is enabled in the AddOns list.

## Notes

World totems must be available to the game's normal mouseover tooltip system. Your nameplate settings can affect which friendly and hostile totems are easy to hover.

Built for WoW: Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). Fire Nova and Tranquil Air are intentionally not listed: in Forever, Fire Nova is no longer a totem and Tranquil Air is not present.
