OPTIONAL LIVE CHECKLIST - build 1.60.1.70170

Version 1.0.2 corrects the displayed title only. The feeding workflow was
owner-tested and accepted for 1.0.0; these remain optional diagnostic checks.

1. Enable the addon on a hunter who knows Feed Pet. /fpf opens settings.
   The minimap button should also open settings on left-click, toggle layout
   preview on right-click, and move around the rim when dragged. Verify its
   position persists through /reload and /fpf minimap off/on hides/restores it.
   Unlock, drag, resize, click Done. /reload should preserve position and size.
   /fpf test nofood previews the red crossed-out icon; /fpf lock finishes.
2. With a living, present, unhappy or content pet, stand out of combat with
   suitable food. The Feed Pet icon should flash. Its tooltip names a food.
3. Left-click once. Exactly one item should be consumed by Feed Pet, and
   the pet should gain its feeding effect. The icon should hide during that
   effect and remain hidden when the pet reaches happy status.
4. Move/sort food in your bags; the next click should still feed suitable food.
   Test eating the last suitable food. On the next needed feed, NO FOOD should
   appear; clicking that warning must not cast anything. Buying suitable food
   should restore the feeding button when feeding is needed.
5. Check a second pet family with a different diet. Unsuitable items must not
   be selected. Check that the lowest-level food still accepted by the native
   API really feeds this beta pet; report any client rejection with /fpf status.
6. Verify no alert while happy, in player combat, in pet-only combat, pet dead,
   dismissed, player dead/ghost, mounted, or riding a griffin/wyvern taxi.
   When normal conditions return, the correct alert should return.
7. Try Shift-drag/wheel and layout preview. They must not consume food.
   Enter combat while previewing; the preview should disappear and stay locked.
8. Check for Lua errors or "action blocked" messages after combat and feeding.
   Include /fpf status output and the exact error when reporting a problem.
