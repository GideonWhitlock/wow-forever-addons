# Artwork record

The project emblem was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 11 October 2026, then converted locally to a 128×128, 32-bit RGBA TGA for WoW.

Final generation prompt:

> Edit the generated emblem for production use as a World of Warcraft minimap button texture. Keep the same original scroll, green leaf curtain/shield, small compass star, antique-gold circular frame, and forest-green palette. Make the outer circular frame perfectly centered and symmetrical with a generous 8% transparent safety margin. Remove every pixel outside the circle to true alpha transparency, including all four corners and any shadow beyond the rim. Simplify tiny scroll details slightly and increase interior contrast so the subject remains legible at 24–32 pixels. No text, letters, logos, watermark, or square backdrop. Preserve the clean circular frame and center the subject optically.

The emblem does not use RestedXP or Blizzard logos, characters, artwork or other brand assets. `artwork/project-logo.png` is the high-resolution source for the project page. `addon/RXPObjectiveHiderForever/Media/Icon.tga` is the in-game texture.

Automated checks cover dimensions, color mode, transparent corners, circular-alpha symmetry, subject centering and small-size contrast. The owner accepted the installed in-game presentation for release.

The CurseForge project image uses a wide safe margin so the complete circular emblem remains visible in square and circular thumbnail crops.
