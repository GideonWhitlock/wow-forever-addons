MINIMAP BUTTON FOREVER - LIVE TEST CHECKLIST

VERSION 1.0.23 INTERRUPTED-DRAG CHECK

1. Hold Alt and drag the launcher away from the minimap.
2. While dragging, open the world map or enter combat so WoW can interrupt the
   normal OnDragStop event.
3. Release the left mouse button and Alt.
4. Move the cursor and close the interrupting UI.

Expected:
- The launcher stops following the cursor as soon as Alt or the left mouse
  button is released.
- It remains at the saved position and does not shoot toward the cursor or snap
  back to the minimap rim.

VERSION 1.0.22 SECRET-GEOMETRY CHECK
- With the add-on that creates the unnamed `Bar.lua:63` button enabled, scan
  before combat, during ordinary combat and after combat. Confirm no secret-value
  error occurs and that the restricted button is not collected.
- Confirm ordinary 8-80 pixel add-on minimap buttons still collect, resize and
  return to their original size and position when organisation is disabled.

VERSION 1.0.21 GATHERLITE PIN CHECK
- With GatherLite enabled and nearby herb or mining nodes visible on the minimap,
  click Scan buttons. Confirm no numbered GatherLite resource pins enter the bar.
- Confirm the genuine GatherLite launcher remains collected and clickable.

VERSION 1.0.19 EXCLUSION CHECK
- Open settings, choose exclusions, and tick a named chat/message minimap button.
  Confirm it returns to its original minimap position immediately, remains there
  after Scan buttons and /reload, and still flashes for a new message.
- Untick it and confirm it rejoins the collected bar. Confirm unnamed map pins are
  not presented as persistent exclusions.
